Struycken — Technisch Procédé index3

A faithful re-implementation of the point-digitization procedure devised by Peter Struycken with F.C.A. Groen and P.W. Verbeek (TH Delft) for the 1981 Beatrix stamp, following Paul Hefting's description: a meander scan accumulates darkness and drops a dot each time the sum reaches 255; then the "box of marbles" is shaken — each dot rolls a die, moves one place, may drift at most 3 places from home, and may never touch another dot's territory. Every run is reproducible from its seed.
